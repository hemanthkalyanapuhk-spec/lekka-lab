📄 Lekka Lab Practice Sheet: Number Sense & Place Value (Classes 1 & 2)

Student Name: _______________________ Date: ____________ Max Score: 12
1. Write the number name for $48$: ________________________
2. Fill in the blanks: $76 = $ ______ Tens $+$ ______ Ones.
3. What number comes strictly between $89$ and $91$? Answer: ______
4. Insert $>$ or $<$:   $64$ [   ] $46$   and   $108$ [   ] $180$.
5. Add with carrying:   $47 + 38 = $ _________
6. Money Problem: If a pencil costs ₹5 and an eraser costs ₹3, find the total cost: ₹_________
Answer Key:
1. Forty-eight.
2. $7$ Tens $+$ $6$ Ones ($70 + 6$).
3. $90$.
4. $64 > 46$ and $108 < 180$.
5. Ones: $7 + 8 = 15$ (write $5$, carry $1$). Tens: $4 + 3 + 1 = 8 \implies 85$.
6. Total cost $= \text{₹}5 + \text{₹}3 = \text{₹}8$.

📄 Lekka Lab Practice Sheet: Fractions, LCM & Money (Classes 3, 4 & 5)

Student Name: _______________________ Date: ____________ Max Score: 12
1. Find the prime factorisation of $72$ using the ladder method.
2. Find the LCM of $4$ and $6$: Answer: _________
3. Solve the unlike fraction addition: $\frac{2}{3} + \frac{1}{4} = $ _________
4. Long Division: Divide $95 \div 4$. State the Quotient and Remainder.
5. Convert the improper fraction $\frac{13}{5}$ into a mixed number: _________
6. Money Problem: Rahul has a ₹100 note. He buys a notebook for ₹45 and a geometry box for ₹35. How much balance money does he receive back? Answer: ₹_________
Answer Key:
1. $72 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 3 \times 3 = 2^3 \times 3^2$.
2. Multiples of $4: 4, 8, 12\dots$; Multiples of $6: 6, 12\dots \implies \text{LCM} = 12$.
3. $\frac{8}{12} + \frac{3}{12} = \frac{11}{12}$.
4. $4 \times 23 = 92$; Remainder $= 3 \implies \text{Quotient} = 23, \text{Remainder} = 3$.
5. $13 \div 5 = 2$ with remainder $3 \implies 2\frac{3}{5}$.
6. Total spent $= \text{₹}45 + \text{₹}35 = \text{₹}80$. Balance $= \text{₹}100 - \text{₹}80 = \text{₹}20$.

📄 Lekka Lab Practice Sheet: Integers, Sign Rules & Commercial Math (Classes 6 & 7)

Student Name: _______________________ Date: ____________ Max Score: 12
1. Compute: (a) $(-8) + (-5) = $ ______   (b) $(-12) - (-7) = $ ______
2. Multiply & Divide: (a) $(-6) \times (-4) = $ ______   (b) $(-35) \div (+7) = $ ______
3. Simplify using Exponent Laws: $\frac{2^5 \times 2^3}{2^4} = $ ______
4. Solve for $x$ with transposition: $3x - 7 = 14$.
5. Spot the Error: A student wrote $-5 - 3 = +8$. Explain why this is wrong.
6. Profit & Loss: A shopkeeper buys a school bag for ₹250 and sells it for ₹300. Calculate his profit percentage: _________%
Answer Key:
1. (a) $-13$. (b) $-12 + 7 = -5$.
2. (a) $+24$. (b) $-5$.
3. $2^{5+3-4} = 2^4 = 16$.
4. $3x = 14 + 7 = 21 \implies x = \frac{21}{3} = 7$.
5. In addition of negative numbers, values add and keep negative sign: $-5 - 3 = -8$.
6. $\text{Profit} = \text{₹}300 - \text{₹}250 = \text{₹}50$. $\text{Profit } \% = \left(\frac{50}{250}\right) \times 100 = 20\%$.

📄 Lekka Lab Practice Sheet: Factorisation, Quadratics & Simple Interest (Classes 8, 9 & 10)

Student Name: _______________________ Date: ____________ Max Score: 12
1. Factorise using algebraic identities: $49x^2 - 36y^2 = $ ____________________
2. Split the middle term to factorise: $x^2 + 7x + 12$. State $p$ and $q$.
3. Rationalise the denominator: $\frac{1}{\sqrt{5} - \sqrt{2}} = $ ____________________
4. Find the roots of $2x^2 - 7x + 3 = 0$ using the Quadratic Formula: $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.
5. In right triangle $\triangle ABC$, if $\sin\theta = \frac{3}{5}$, calculate $\cos\theta$ and $\tan\theta$.
6. Simple Interest: Find the Simple Interest (SI) and total amount on a sum of ₹5,000 at $8\%$ per annum for $2$ years: $\text{SI} = $ ₹_________, $\text{Amount} = $ ₹_________
Answer Key:
1. $(7x - 6y)(7x + 6y)$.
2. $p=3, q=4 \implies (x + 3)(x + 4)$.
3. $\frac{\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{2}}{5 - 2} = \frac{\sqrt{5} + \sqrt{2}}{3}$.
4. $D = 49 - 24 = 25 \implies x = \frac{7 \pm 5}{4} \implies x = 3 \text{ or } x = \frac{1}{2}$.
5. $\cos\theta = \frac{4}{5}, \tan\theta = \frac{3}{4}$.
6. $\text{SI} = \frac{5000 \times 8 \times 2}{100} = \text{₹}800$. Total Amount $= \text{₹}5000 + \text{₹}800 = \text{₹}5,800$.
